# BuzzQuiz category catalogue

Reviewed 18 November 2026. The 60 subjects below each have four illustrated outcomes and one example basis. The basis is the part the player answers questions about. For example, a quiz can reveal a pasta shape by asking how someone tackles a problem.

## Where the subjects came from

BuzzFeed published take counts for 100 individual quizzes from the 2010s. Its leading results included US state (48,834,284), city (23,168,790), career (22,383,203), picky eating (19,318,895) and a Hogwarts house sorting quiz (17,964,032). Dog breed reached 12,808,394 and decade reached 10,129,995. These are historical counts for specific quizzes. Food can play either role in this product. A food-as-personality quiz reached 9,877,592, and several entries used food preferences to predict something else. [BuzzFeed’s decade ranking](https://www.buzzfeed.com/andrewziegler/best-buzzfeed-quizzes)

BuzzFeed’s public Trending page also featured romance, desserts, bird personalities, Hogwarts houses, geography and general knowledge in the snapshot reviewed. Trivia is a popular format with a different scoring model. Animal, fandom and place subjects translate directly into BuzzQuiz’s four-outcome format. [BuzzFeed Trending Quizzes](https://www.buzzfeed.com/trending/quizzes/)

QuizCheese’s homepage Top list showed haircut decision, city to live in, pasta shape, boundaries with in-laws and cheese, in that order. Hair, city, pasta and boundaries also appeared under Trending. The page showed question counts but no play totals. For BuzzQuiz, a haircut decision becomes four hairstyle results. Family boundaries can supply a question basis for a communication-style quiz. [QuizCheese homepage](https://www.quizcheese.com/)

Opus 5.5 contributed eras, aesthetics, cosmic objects, occupations and social roles. GPT-6.2 Sol contributed urban birds, tide pools, stationery, houseplants and behavioural question bases. Cursor reported an exhausted usage allowance before Grok 4.7 could return findings. The catalogue merges overlapping suggestions and adds subjects such as creator types and college majors. Its order groups related artwork. The narrower categories are editorial ideas whose demand in BuzzQuiz is unmeasured.

## Using the artwork

Each linked sheet is a 4 × 4 transparent WebP. Each row follows the subject order above, and each column follows the outcome order from left to right. The [machine-readable catalogue](../src/lib/category-portraits.json) includes a visual description per cell. The quiz library can resolve a matching category and outcome locally. A name shared across categories goes through the existing writer review. Users can still request outcomes outside this collection.
